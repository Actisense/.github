# Actisense — Convert · Digitise · Share

Welcome to Actisense on GitHub! We share code, examples, documentation and more with our valued customers and the wider developer community.

Explore our award-winning range of sensors and interfaces at **[actisense.com](https://www.actisense.com)**.

All of our digital products expose powerful APIs (Application Programming Interfaces), and this site shares the code you need to integrate them into your own projects. Whether you're a large system integrator building a fleet-management platform or a keen sailor who wants to unlock your vessel's full potential — you're in the right place!
